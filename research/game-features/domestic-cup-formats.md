Researched 2026-11-07 by reading the Wikipedia page for each cup ([DFB-Pokal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DFB-Pokal), [Coppa Italia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppa_Italia), [Copa del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_del_Rey), [EFL Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Cup)), plus the earlier web searches in [competition-formats.md](competition-formats.md). **How reliable this is:** the fetch tool returned the first ~39k characters of each page verbatim and a machine summary of the rest. Facts below marked **[summary]** come only from that summary. For the EFL Cup the last ~46k characters were never read, so its entry points and rule history are **[summary]** only. No page gives its own snapshot date. Wikipedia pages are secondary sources; the federation rulebooks were not read.

## DFB-Pokal

- **Entrants (64):** all 36 clubs of the Bundesliga and 2. Bundesliga, the top 4 of the 3. Liga, 21 regional cup winners, and 3 places for the regional associations with the most men's teams (usually the runner-up of the association cup). Reserve teams are not allowed (since 2008).
- **Draw:** round 1 uses two pots of 32. Pot 1: the 18 Bundesliga clubs plus the top 14 of the 2. Bundesliga. Pot 2: the bottom 4 of the 2. Bundesliga, the top 4 of the 3. Liga and the 24 amateur teams. A pot 1 club is always drawn against a pot 2 club. **The pot-2 team (the amateur or lower club) hosts**, since 1982. Round 2 uses the same pot principle; once one pot is empty the rest are drawn together and the first-drawn team hosts. Later rounds are drawn from one pot.
- **Ties:** extra time after 90 minutes, then penalties. No replays (finals since 1977, all games since 1991).
- **No format change** is described for 2014-15 to 2023-24.
- **Not stated:** number of rounds, byes, regional sections, away goals.

## Coppa Italia

- **Rounds (7), from the entry table:**

| Round | Clubs | New entrants |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary (August) | 8 | the lowest-ranked 8: 4 from Serie B, 4 from Serie C |
| First | 32 | 12 Serie A clubs and 16 Serie B clubs, plus the 4 preliminary winners (the Serie A ones are the lowest-ranked, unless they are in Europe) |
| Second | 16 | none |
| Round of 16 | 16 | the top 8 Serie A clubs (ranked 1 to 8) |
| Quarter-finals, semi-finals, final | 8, 4, 2 | none |

  The page itself is inconsistent here (the text calls the top-8 entry the "third round", and some table columns don't add up), so the sizes above were worked out from the "clubs involved" column. Treat the round sizes as likely but not certain.
- **Draw:** the pairings for every round are made in advance; the whole bracket is drawn before the first match. Seeding details are not stated.
- **Ties:** through 2023-24, extra time after every draw at 90 minutes. **From 2024-25, extra time only in the semi-finals and the final; every other round goes straight to penalties.**
- **Team count over time:** the current page says 44 clubs. Earlier sources I found mentioned 78 clubs (Sportmonks and others). When the field was cut is **not stated**, so **the 2014-2023 team count is unknown here**.

## Copa del Rey

- **Reform from 2019-20 (stated on the page):** every round becomes single-leg with the **lower-division team hosting**, except the semi-finals (two legs). Before that, ties were two-legged from the last 32, where the top clubs entered. Entrants rose from 83 to 125 (including fifth-level regional winners), and later 126 in 2025.
- **Entry:** most top-level clubs enter in the first round; the four Supercopa de España clubs enter in the third round (last 32).
- **Final:** one match at a neutral venue, Estadio de La Cartuja in Seville, chosen for four years from 2019-20 and now described as the regular home.
- **Supercopa link:** from 2019-20 the Copa champion and runner-up join the top two league finishers.
- **Europa League spot:** the winner qualifies; "until 2014" it was the runner-up (the page doesn't say which season exactly).
- **Not stated:** how a draw is decided in a single-leg round, away goals, number of rounds, byes, draw procedure. An unconfirmed web result says the competition expands to 116 teams in 2026/27; the Wikipedia page gives 126 and I did not use the 116.

## EFL Cup (Carabao Cup)

- **Seven rounds, 92 clubs.** The first two rounds are split into North and South sections. A bye system based on league level lets higher-ranked clubs enter later and delays clubs in Europe. Every round except the final is a random draw.
- **Entry [summary]:** most Football League clubs in round 1; remaining Premier League clubs in round 2; clubs in European competition get a bye to round 3. A preliminary round exists only in some seasons (2002-03, 2011-12, 2025-26, 2026-27). About 32 teams remain by round 3.
- **Legs:** single-leg ties, except the semi-finals (home and away). Final at Wembley.
- **Rule history [summary]:** extra time was scrapped from 2018-19 for every round except the final, and the away-goals rule was dropped in the semi-finals, so level ties go to penalties; the final goes to penalties after extra time (since 1998, replays before that); 2020-21 semi-finals were single-leg because of the pandemic; round 1 and 2 were two-legged until 2000-01.
- **Name:** called the EFL Cup from 2016-17 (the Football League became the EFL); the sponsor's name (Carabao) is separate.
- **Not stated:** home advantage in the draw. Earlier web searches agreed there are no replays.

## What this means for our engine

Everything above fits the stage model in [competition-design.md](../engineering/competition-design.md). A `Knockout` stage needs these parameters, and each differs per cup and per season:

| Parameter | Examples |
|---|---|
| Entry round per club group | Coppa Italia: top 8 Serie A enter at the round of 16. EFL Cup: Premier League clubs in Europe enter at round 3. |
| Legs per round | all single except semi-finals (Coppa Italia, Copa del Rey, EFL Cup); Copa del Rey two-legged from the last 32 before 2019-20 |
| How a level tie ends, per round | extra time then penalties (DFB-Pokal); penalties only except semis and final (Coppa Italia from 2024-25); penalties only except the final (EFL Cup from 2018-19) |
| Replays | none in any of these four; FA Cup had them before 2024-25 |
| Draw style | two pots with the lower club at home (DFB-Pokal); whole bracket in advance (Coppa Italia); random each round (EFL Cup); regional sections (EFL Cup rounds 1 and 2) |
| Home advantage rule | lower division hosts (DFB-Pokal, Copa del Rey); first-drawn hosts |
| Final | neutral venue in all four |
| European place | winner gets Europa League or Conference League; if already qualified, the place goes to the next-best league finisher |

Season changes found inside our 2014-2023 window: Copa del Rey reform (2019-20), EFL Cup extra time removed (2018-19), EFL Cup 2020-21 single-leg semi-finals, Coppa Italia penalty rule (2024-25, just after the window). The DFB-Pokal has none.
